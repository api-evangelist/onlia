High risk auto insurance in Ontario can feel like you’re stuck in a penalty box: prices jump, fewer insurers want to provide coverage, and renewals become stressful. fortunately, “high risk” isn’t a label forever. It’s a pricing and eligibility category based on measurable factors, and there are practical ways to stabilize your coverage and reduce costs over time.
