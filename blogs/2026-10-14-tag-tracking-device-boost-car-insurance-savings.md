A TAG tracking anti theft device is a vehicle recovery product that may help Ontario drivers meet an insurer’s anti-theft requirement or qualify for a theft-prevention credit when one is available. It is installed in your vehicle and used to help locate it if it is stolen. Onlia can help you understand where an anti-theft device fits into an Ontario auto insurance quote, without a sales call or guesswork.
